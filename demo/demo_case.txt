Case ID: DEMO-001
Category: Connectivity
Severity: High
Reported By: Network Operations Team
Date: 2024-01-15

--- ISSUE DESCRIPTION ---

Users in the Chicago branch office are experiencing complete loss of connectivity
to the corporate data center in New York. The outage started at approximately
09:15 AM local time and is affecting all 120 users in the branch.

Symptoms:
- Ping to 10.10.0.1 (default gateway) succeeds
- Ping to 192.168.1.1 (DC core router) fails
- Traceroute times out after the third hop
- No BGP route updates received in the last 2 hours
- Branch router logs show: "BGP neighbor 203.0.113.1 went down - Hold timer expired"

Environment:
- Branch Router: Cisco ISR 4431 (IOS XE 17.6)
- WAN: MPLS circuit via Provider X (Circuit ID: AX-2024-CH-001)
- Redundant link: None (single MPLS link)

--- END OF CASE ---
